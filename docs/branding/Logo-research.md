# SponsorEvidence logo research and options

Three logo directions for a sports-media evidence platform. Each option has a full SponsorEvidence wordmark, a standalone icon, white artwork for dark surfaces, and 32 px and 64 px favicon samples. The comparison board uses the website’s stadium art, colors and header scale.

## What the research informed

- **Put the craft into the letterforms.** Pentagram refined Liberty’s original sign into a logotype and treated its crest as an optional element. The SponsorEvidence serif options use the site’s DM Serif Display, with adjusted spacing around the capital E and other tight letter pairs. The shape and spacing carry the editorial character. [Pentagram’s Liberty identity](https://www.pentagram.com/work/liberty)
- **Make the icon work in a broadcast frame.** Premier League’s type designers developed a wordmark into a system for mobile, TV and print, with attention to distinguishable small details. These options each have one compact icon independent of the long name. [Monotype’s Premier League case study](https://www.monotype.com/resources/case-studies/premier-league)
- **Treat reversed artwork as a proper variant.** UEFA documents one-color versions for cases where full-color artwork is impractical. Every SponsorEvidence direction has the same silhouette in evergreen and white. The dark-stadium preview places the mark over a shaded crop for legibility. [UEFA logo guidelines](https://www.uefa.com/MultimediaFiles/MediaGraphics/mediadownload/MediaDownload/02/40/68/00/2406800_MEDIAGRAPHICS.pdf)
- **Check the browser tab separately.** Google specifies a square favicon and recommends an asset larger than 48 px for search surfaces. The board checks visual legibility at 16 px and 32 px, and the deliverable includes a 64 px favicon for larger placements. [Google Search Central favicon guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/favicon-in-search/)

## The three directions

| Direction | Design | Best fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proof Frame | A wide and a tall frame share a seam. The chamfered upper corner gives the symbol a recognizable silhouette. The wordmark uses DM Serif Display. | Strongest match for the site’s paired replays and the product’s comparison workflow. |
| Editorial Seal | Interlocking S and E initials form a signature for a reviewed report. The serif wordmark is spaced a little more tightly. | The most luxurious and human-feeling direction, especially for report covers and evidence exports. |
| Touchline | The pitch’s center circle and halfway line form a compact icon. A Manrope wordmark gives the shape room and reads distinctly at header size. | The clearest sports reference and a strong icon for mobile. |

**Recommendation.** Proof Frame makes the product’s purpose visible and fits the Evidence Atlas visual language. Editorial Seal is the alternative if the brand should feel like a sports-media publisher.

## Asset preparation

The built-in ImageGen tool produced one transparent icon study per direction. The final delivery translates the forms into clean one-color vectors, with custom spacing and exact lettering. The icon conformance check rendered all three at 16, 32 and 64 px in Chromium. The illustrated dark-surface test uses the actual SponsorEvidence stadium image. Each SVG is standalone and the wordmarks are outlined, so they display without a font installation.

## ImageGen prompts

### Proof Frame

Use case: logo-brand. Generate exactly one original, extremely restrained SponsorEvidence logomark icon only, called Proof Frame. Square 1024x1024 composition, centered with clean generous padding, truly transparent background. One solid ink-green color #102C23, no gradients, no shadows, no outline of canvas. Design an elegant brand symbol made of TWO distinct overlapping recording formats: one flatter landscape evidence frame and one taller portrait evidence frame, perfectly axis aligned, linked across a deliberate central shared seam. Use strong confident strokes, open corners and large knockouts so the icon is clear as a 16px favicon. Visually more ownable than a stock scan-corners icon: one outer contour is strongly rectangular and asymmetric, one vertical inner bay is the second scene. Slight custom optical balance in line weights and terminal cuts; it feels drawn by an experienced identity designer. Single standalone symbol, clean vector-like silhouette. No letters, no type, no arrows, no checkmarks, no grids, no mockup, no extra marks, no texture.

### Editorial Seal

Use case: logo-brand. One original SponsorEvidence monogram only, called Editorial Seal, as an elegant hand-designed brand symbol. 1024x1024 square artwork, truly transparent background, centered strong silhouette with generous outside clear space. The only letters are “SE” in one tightly interlocked custom editorial ligature. Draw them in solid deep ink evergreen #102C23. Use precisely shaped curved S, decisive open E bars with bracketed terminal detail, graceful moderate stroke contrast and thicker strokes anywhere that would disappear on small displays. The S and E share optical space and may share a counter, with enough breathing room to read both instantly. Sophisticated publisher insignia, quietly luxurious, not delicate or ornamental. Optically balanced and human-drawn; white space of the symbol transparent. Only a single two-letter mark; no surrounding crest or outline, no gold, no gradients, no decorations, no page mockup, no spelled-out wordmark.

### Touchline

Use case: logo-brand. Generate one standalone original icon for SponsorEvidence, called Touchline. Square 1024x1024 artwork, transparent background, ample clear space around a single deep ink evergreen (#102C23) mark. Draw a geometrically confident, compact emblem inspired by the halfway line and center circle on an overhead football pitch. Visually interpret the circle and central line as a small interruption crossing two complementary filled half shapes, like pitch paint observed in a broadcast frame. It should be memorable and near-square at favicon scale, intentional like a refined human-designed sports publishing mark. Open negative space with thick reserved outline where needed. Front-facing axis aligned 2D only. Avoid literal ball, field diagram, fake team crest, gradients, status checkmarks, leaves, cameras, trophy, letterforms, text, watermark, or mockup. One clean vector-like shape only, flat color silhouette and transparent knockouts.
