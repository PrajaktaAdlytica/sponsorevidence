# SponsorEvidence market research and motion direction

SponsorEvidence’s most credible opening is a feed-specific placement audit that the buyer can compare with an existing reporting process. The site should let someone inspect the agreement, compare the recordings and make a simulated review decision before asking for a meeting. That is the central product story and the primary reason to invest in scroll-driven design.

Research reviewed on 25 November 2026. Live visual observations below are desktop samples. Publication and mobile behavior are identified separately where applicable.

The market sources establish product capabilities vendors describe. They do not establish what these vendors can reconcile in a given rights holder’s environment. Which system can resolve the exact case and how much reviewer effort it takes are discovery questions.

### What the buyer can resolve today

| Current input | What it may establish | What to inspect with the buyer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exposure dashboard | Observed mark, visible seconds, position, feed or asset metrics where provided | Can it show the exact authorized recording and replay window and carry the schedule requirement into a reviewable finding? |
| Signed terms and placement schedule | Which sponsor, delivery feed, event, position and conditions were agreed | How are amendments approved? Which term version binds the event? Can operators map the term to feed IDs consistently? |
| Manually annotated clips | Why a human judged the placement delivered, missing or ambiguous | How much time does it take to find and align versions, document coverage gaps and explain an exception in a recap? |
| Reconciled delivery report | The team’s current conclusion and sponsor-facing evidence policy | Which cases were missed, escalated or reopened? What artifacts would satisfy the reviewer and commercial owner? |

**First buyer.** Head of Partnership Reporting at a sports rights holder or sponsorship measurement agency. Confirm the rights holder’s budget owner and who can sign, renew and provide the relevant scope. Analysts and account managers handle evidence. Measurement operations maintains artwork, schedules and coverage, and approves exports. An assigned reviewer confirms detection and interpretation. Commercial staff decide make-good or sponsor-facing distribution. Separate feed and artwork owners can authorize use.

**Demand test.** Start through sports marketing agencies and media delivery partners with the annotated fictional contract-to-clip case. Ask the workflow owner to bring a prior dispute and compare the incumbent process. Seek a paid commitment, schedule, applicable terms, authorized feeds, approved marks and a named reviewer. Evaluate whether the buyer would fund Placement Audit alongside the incumbent before widening the product.

**Hypotheses.** Commercial fit, integration, model accuracy, pricing, paid commitments and renewal remain to be tested. Twenty-four equivalent customers at $42,000 per year would produce $1.008 million in annual recurring revenue. That arithmetic is a planning hypothesis. Test buyer reach, renewal and delivery effort before forecasting. Explore permissioned placement-type and logo-variant libraries with production and measurement partners after scope and rights are confirmed.

**Portfolio distinction.** The supplied brief frames Droneza (B09) as incident evidence preservation. SponsorEvidence is about the delivery of agreed visibility in licensed sports footage. That distinction belongs in the internal portfolio handoff.

**Naming and domain.** SponsorEvidence is the current name. The supplied brief records no registry result for legacy sponsorfacet.com on 24 November 2026. Perplexity could not retrieve a current registry response for either name. Domain status and trademark clearance remain unverified. Keep these checks out of launch copy. ## Brand territories

| Direction | What it would look like | Best use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Broadcast Control Room** | Dim production surfaces, crisp timecode, frame guides and compact evidence. Main character is the synchronized replay pair. | Demo and Placement Audit. |
| **Editorial Matchday** | Warm off-white, field-green, large sports photography, a literary serif and a page rhythm that alternates cinematic and quiet scenes. Main character is a sequence of match stills. | Homepage and use-case pages. |
| **Evidence Dossier** | Paper, graphite rules, carefully typeset excerpts, a single amber review accent and vertical disclosure of source and decision. Main character is the signed clause beside the record. | Trust, evaluation and Sponsor Report. |

**Selected direction.** Use Editorial Matchday as the brand foundation. Use the control room for the live evidence chapter and the dossier for detailed proof pages. The dominant public surface stays light. The workbench goes dark so source footage and selection states have a distinct environment.

### Brand foundation for design

- **Logo concept.** Two overlapping open viewfinder frames share one corner. The smaller vertical frame reads as the alternate feed. Use a solid single-color mark with a custom SponsorEvidence wordmark. Test flat black and white, transparent backgrounds and 16 px legibility.
- **Favicon.** Reduce the two frames to one thick corner and a second offset corner. Build an original monochrome SVG and PNG variants. Use the same mark for touch and social assets.
- **Palette.** Warm paper **#F7F5EF**, white **#FFFFFF**, pitch charcoal **#111714**, workbench graphite **#1A211E**, deep field green **#174B39**, warm lime **#C5E67A** for selected dark-surface cues, and amber ink **#805609** on pale amber **#FAEBC4** for pending states. Keep color secondary to text and icons in statuses. Verify every text and control combination at design QA.
- **Type.** Instrument Serif for short editorial display lines and occasional pull text. Geist or Instrument Sans for navigation, body, product headings and UI. IBM Plex Mono for timecodes and IDs. Use a sans H1 where the full tagline needs more compact line breaks. Set the homepage headline in two or three lines depending on width, keep body text readable and use tabular figures in the workbench.
- **UI materials.** Fine graphite dividers, 8–12 px corners on evidence panels, a low shadow beneath report sheets and consistently visible focus. Keep the geometry of the 16:9 and 9:16 feeds exact. For report screens, make source date, version and reviewer state as legible as any metric.
- **Imagery.** Original fictional clubs, players, kits, stadium and sponsor identity. Produce one replay sequence and derive each feed from that sequence for visual continuity. Design the clean and branded graphics as separate original layers. Supply stills, right-to-use source notes and a transcript or concise description for any clip used on the site.

### Motion specifications

- Use short one-time entrance motion for nonessential elements. Start public text in its final readable opacity, and reserve movement for the image mask, guides or UI layers. Start modestly at 180–240 ms for feedback and 350–600 ms for a staged reveal.
- On desktop, enhance the four-module chapter at about 1100 px and above if the viewport height fits it. Use a sticky viewport, normalized document scroll progress and a translate track. Keep focusable links in reading order and ensure focusing a panel makes it visible. Use a simple vertical or native swipe deck when sticky behavior is impractical. No page-wide horizontal overflow.
- Keep the hero’s first frame lightweight. Size media up front, defer below-fold video, pause off-screen playback, leave the replay paused by default on mobile and honor reduced motion with still states. Keep playback, motion pause, keyboard focus and the skip link independent.
- Use hover/focus to reveal source emphasis, show a light depth change on buttons and move an arrow slightly on text links. Design visible pressed, focus, selected, loading, empty, error and success states. Keep any task-critical text visible on touch.
- Check contrast, keyboard order, focus in dialogs, caption/description, touch interaction, narrow screen readability and pause controls. Use W3C guidance to check animation and motion alternatives.
